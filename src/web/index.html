Backends

Models

Hidden models are left out of /v1/models and /api/tags, and requests for them get 404 model not found at once. A rule can apply to every backend or only some — then the model is still served by the others.

Clients

Registered clients authenticate with their API keys and keep their settings across restarts. Unregistered ids come from X-User-ID (or the client's IP) and are shown while active. Open a client to manage its keys. A client or key marked redacted keeps its log entries but stores no request/response text; any single request can opt out the same way by sending the X-Redact-Log header. Requests send their key as Authorization: Bearer <key> or X-Api-Key: <key>.

Settings

Changes are saved into appconf.yaml itself; the previous version is kept as appconf.yaml.bak for “Undo last save”. Saving rewrites the file, so its comments do not survive it. Most apply at once; the ones marked restart wait for a restart. Backends are managed on the Backends page.
