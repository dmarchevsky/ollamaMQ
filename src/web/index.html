Models

Hidden models are left out of /v1/models and /api/tags, and requests for them get 404 model not found at once. A rule can apply to every backend or only some — then the model is still served by the others.

Clients

Registered clients authenticate with their API keys and keep their settings across restarts. Unregistered ids come from X-User-ID (or the client's IP) and are shown while active.

Settings
